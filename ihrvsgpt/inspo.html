What is this Series / Inspiration

Growing up following soccer in the US, I remember the BBC sports website was always my main source of news and analysis. I particularly remember the weekly “Lawro vs. Guests” series. I had no idea who Mark Lawrenson was nor did I always rate his insights but it was a fun column to read. Basically each week he and a different “celebrity” guest predicted the scores of all 10 matches. A correct scoreline was worth 3 points and a correct result (without the right score) was worth 1 point.

After the World Cup ended I wanted to continue with my higher-than-typical level of engagement with football for the upcoming season. My idea was that I would recreate this series but with two different opponents – random guests, and also AI (chatGPT). Upon coming up with this idea, I decided to see if Lawro was still going but saw that he ended the series four years ago. Unfortunately scouring the BBC website I also saw that they have revived the series and had the same exact idea – to have the analyst (Chris Sutton in this case) go up against AI. Despite my idea being seemingly unoriginal I still think it will be fun to commit to the challenge and see how I do.

Ahead of this past summer I found myself not as excited for the World Cup as I would’ve expected just given I felt more allegiance to my club team than any particular national team (given the USMNT are not very good and I hadn’t even bothered going through the paperwork to secure my British passport). But as soon as the tournament started and I ended up getting tickets to quite a few of the games it was hard imagining returning to the “marathon” season-long club scrap vs. the excitement and importance of every World Cup game. Coupling that with Pep Guardiola departing the club (and a very unexciting replacement), large personnel changes (Rodri and Bernardo Silva in particular along with constant rumors around signing maybe the most hated player in England Enzo Fernandez), and a really pathetic display in the FA Community Shield – this all had really sapped a lot of my enthusiasm as we enter this upcoming Premier League season. I unfortunately think this season is going to be a rough one for Manchester City. So I’m hoping the charges aren’t too severe and that this weekly “blog” will keep me motivated through the season.
